Limitations

Sitters can take care of the account when the owner is not being able to play. They can use basic menus like building structures and research sciences or to send fleets on a Space Trip mission where they can be safe from battles.

Note that Sitters have some restrictions when using the account of the owner to prevent abuses. Most important is that they cannot spend owner's Credits and also cannot see incoming Flights to owner's planets.
